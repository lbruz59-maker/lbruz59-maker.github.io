Coast Rush HD V668

Rebuilt vehicle artwork
- Renault Clio, Volkswagen Golf GTI Mk1 and Mercedes-Benz E55 AMG W211 have new transparent 4x5 sprite sheets.
- Each sheet contains 20 complete-car frames: five rear yaw angles (hard left, light left, neutral straight rear, light right, hard right) and four wheel-animation phases.
- The neutral straight-rear view is centered in the garage by default. The game maps steering/drift to matching rear angles and tilts the car on uphill/downhill road grades.
- The Clio neutral rear frame receives a small width normalization so its silhouette matches the Golf and Mercedes.
- Silver body panels use a dedicated recoloring mask; dark glass, tail lamps, tires and wheel zones remain separate. Garage color is rendered through WebGL 2.
- The original OutRun Ferrari sprites and animation path are unchanged.
