Coast Rush HD V665

Vehicle update
- 21 vehicles now use complete-car sprite atlases with 5 rear views and 4 baked animation phases per view.
- Mercedes, Porsche 911, Golf GTI and Clio use the newly authored transparent sprite sheets.
- The original OutRun Ferrari keeps its original model and renderer.
- Removed the runtime wheel masks, wheel cutouts and shader wheel replacement passes.
- Garage previews now crop the matching vehicle sheet and keep its natural proportions.
- Silver body paint can be recolored without tinting the red brake lamps.

World update
- Regional tunnel portal sprites are stretched to panoramic screen width while their central opening stays tied to the road width.
- Added more route-specific trees, rocks and buildings, with clearance around tunnels, bridges and loops.
- Enabled region-colored bridge profiles on desert routes as well as other standard routes.
- Rendering remains on the native WebGL 2 path.
